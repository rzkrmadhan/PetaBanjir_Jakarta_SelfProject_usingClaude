Where Jakarta floods most often

Number of flood records per kelurahan (neighborhood), 2013 to 2020, from BPBD DKI Jakarta reports. Darker means flooded more often. Tap or hover a neighborhood for details.

Most often flooded

    How to read this. This shows past flooding, not a forecast. A count is the number of flood records BPBD logged for that kelurahan (one flood can create several records if several RW were hit). Bigger neighborhoods with more RW can get higher counts. Kepulauan Seribu is not shown.
    Classes are fixed cut points (0, 1 to 5, 6 to 15, 16 to 35, 36 and up) so the map can be compared with other years later. Colors are colorblind safe.
    Sources. Flood records: BPBD DKI Jakarta via Open Data Jakarta, combined per kelurahan in dashboard-banjir-jakarta. Boundaries: OpenStreetMap (same repo). Map projection: Web Mercator.
